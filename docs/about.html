About Me

As a Graphic Design student at California State University, East Bay, I explore the intersection of art and digital storytelling. My creative journey began at Modesto Junior College, where I earned an associate's degree in Studio Art and began learning Adobe Design software. Influenced by online communities on platforms like YouTube and Discord, I like draw inspiration from the gaming world, particularly its characters, environments, and visual storytelling. I also like painting in my free time.
